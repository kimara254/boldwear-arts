ARTIST STATEMENT

Our stories are not static.
They are still becoming.

BoldWearArts is the independent art practice of Simon Kaguru Kimara, working under the name BoldWearArts. The practice explores the meeting point of cultural memory, ancestral knowledge, landscapes, historical experience and contemporary African identity.

Through digital art and AI-assisted, painterly mixed-media processes, each work uses symbolism, texture, form and metaphor to consider the tensions between tradition and transformation. The intention is not to freeze African culture in the past, but to engage it as a living, evolving presence.

Rooted in East African experience and open to wider perspectives, the work invites reflection and connection across borders.
